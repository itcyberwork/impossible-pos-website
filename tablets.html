One-Time Tablet Setup

How to Download the App to Your Tablet Desktop

Add the official Impossible POS icon to your tablet home screen. It runs in full-screen kiosk mode with zero browser address bars or navigation buttons.

Android Tablets (Samsung, Lenovo, Sunmi)

Google Chrome Browser
  1. 1 Open Google Chrome on your Android tablet.
  2. 2 Tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top right corner.
  3. 3 Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. 4 Done! The official Impossible POS app icon will appear on your desktop.

Apple iPads (iPad Mini, Air, Pro)

Apple Safari Browser
  1. 1 Open Apple Safari on your iPad.
  2. 2 Tap the Share button (box with an arrow pointing up).
  3. 3 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" (+).
  4. 4 Tap Add in the top right. It opens full screen with zero borders.
