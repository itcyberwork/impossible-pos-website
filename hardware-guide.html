3 Thermal Printers & Cash Drawer Setup

Printers

USB ESC/POS Printers (80mm / 58mm)

1. Connect your thermal receipt printer via USB cable.
2. Install the manufacturer driver (Epson, Star, MUNBYN, Rongta, etc.).
3. Run Option 11 in the setup installer to enable the background print service.
4. In POS, open Settings > Hardware and choose your receipt printer.

Cash Drawer

Automatic Cash Drawer Kick (RJ11)

1. Connect the RJ11 cable from the bottom of the cash drawer into the back of your receipt printer (DK port).
2. When a cash sale is completed, the receipt printer automatically triggers the electrical pulse to pop the drawer open.
3. Manual drawer kick button is available anytime on the POS top navigation bar.

4 Network Checklist & Troubleshooting

Same Local Network (WiFi / Ethernet)
All kitchen monitors, tablets, and kiosks must be connected to the same internet router as the Main Register PC.
Firewall Ports 3000 & 8080
The setup file automatically allows ports 3000 and 8080 through Windows Firewall so screens communicate freely.
Instant Re-Pairing
If you move a screen or change routers, simply open /pair.html on the screen to pair again with a fresh 4-digit code.
