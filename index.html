Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Impossible POS.

Do I need to buy proprietary hardware?

Not at all. We believe in open ecosystems. Impossible POS runs flawlessly on your existing Windows PCs, Apple iPads, Android Tablets, and standard Epson or Star Micronics thermal printers.

What happens if my internet goes down?

Your restaurant won't skip a beat. Our system is built with a resilient offline-first architecture. You can continue ringing up orders and printing tickets, and the system will sync with the cloud automatically once connection is restored.

Are there hidden fees for Kitchen Displays (KDS)?

No. Unlike our competitors who charge you per screen, we include unlimited KDS and Line Buster tablet connections for free. You only pay for the main cash register terminals.

Is my data secure?

Extremely. We offer Biometric Fingerprint authentication for your employees and direct NVR/DVR security camera integrations to completely eliminate shrinkage and unauthorized access.
